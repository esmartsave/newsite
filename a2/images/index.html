What is coding photography

This is a process of changing or converting visual photos or images into compact digital formats to save storage space and enhance transmission through networks.

It involves the following:

  1. Photography using code - detecting faces, objects in images
  2. Coding in photography research - is the analyization and categorization of photographs in relation to the specifics features, topics, themes or patterns in relation to subject, colors, composition and emotions.
