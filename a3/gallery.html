What is coding photography

This is a process of changing or converting visual photos or images into compact digital formats to save storage space and enhance transmission through networks.

It involves the following:

  1. Photography using code - detecting faces, objects in images
  2. Coding in photography research - is the analyization and categorization of photographs in relation to the specifics features, topics, themes or patterns in relation to subject, colors, composition and emotions.

Code and photography

The following aspects can be identified:

  1. Visual composition - detecting faces, objects in images
  2. Colors - - gold, horizon, orange, amber, green, black, grey and blue. By identification of this backgrounds, we can know what kind of emotions are likely created.
  3. Coding aspects - - golden hour(box-shadow), pine needles (background-texture, repeating), depth(overlapping of branches/layers - z-index), clean sky(fliter: blur), asymmetry: margin-left: auto;orange or blue(css - warm/cool), focal point, negative space, foreground/mid-ground/background/(z-indexing), field depth(blur), contrast, asymmetry(repeats, after, before).
